Cron is a distraction-free HIIT and interval timer built for high visibility and effortless control.

Inspired by gym wall timers, Cron emphasizes massive, glanceable numbers that you can easily read from across the room even during exhausting workouts.

Features:
* 5 Versatile Timer Modes:
  - INTERVALS: Alternating work and rest periods with round counts
  - AMRAP: As Many Rounds As Possible within a fixed time window
  - EMOM: Every Minute On the Minute with configurable intervals
  - FOR TIME: Countdown to beat your target time
  - STOPWATCH: Precision open-ended timing with lap/checkpoint logging
* High-Contrast Visibility: Pure black OLED contrast with screen-filling numbers designed for quick glances.
* Intuitive Gesture Controls: Adjust intervals effortlessly with a tactile thumbwheel, and control workouts with natural gestures like long-press to pause.
* Sound & Vibration Cues: Distinct audio beeps and vibration cues that guide your pace through your workout music without interrupting voiceovers.
* Privacy & Independence: 100% offline. Zero network permissions requested or used. No ads, tracking, or telemetry.
