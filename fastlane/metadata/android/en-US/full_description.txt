Cron is a distraction-free, privacy-focused HIIT and interval timer built for high visibility and fluid physical motion.

Inspired by gym wall timers, Cron emphasizes big, easy-to-read numbers that you can see from across the room even during exhausting workouts.

Features:
* Multiple Timer Modes:
  - FOR TIME: Countdown to beat your target time
  - AMRAP: As Many Rounds As Possible within a fixed time window
  - EMOM: Every Minute On the Minute with configurable intervals
  - INTERVALS: Alternating work and rest intervals
  - STOPWATCH: Open-ended precision timing with lap/checkpoint logging
* High Visibility: Large timer face maximizing screen real estate for glanceable tracking.
* Tactile & Audible Feedback: Low-latency procedural audio cues and responsive haptic pulses.
* Offline & Private: 100% offline. Zero network permissions requested or used. No ads, tracking, or telemetry.
* Fluid Motion: Physics-based fluid interactions and gesture controls.
